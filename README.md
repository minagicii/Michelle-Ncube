**📍 lawoflargenumbers.py**

*Project that mimics a dice by generating random numbers between 1 and 6.*
- Allows users to scale the number of rolls 
-Results show that data converges to the theoretical probability (1/6) , verifying the law of large numbers.

**📍 random walk simulator for financial modelling.py**

*Simplified model mimicking the methods used in quant finance to forecast price behaviour.*
- Runs 100 separate simulations, that each take 100 random steps determined by whether the random number generator produces a 0 or 1.
- Prints all the ending positions including instances it has been above/below zero.
- Demonstrates unpredictable market behaviour & highlighted the importance of models in option pricing.
